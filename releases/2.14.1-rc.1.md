# Chief of Staff 2.14.1-rc.1

- Permanent resource-change failures persist a correlated review obligation and recover through current source state. Missing requests, identity/reporting gaps and excessive source lineage no longer poison delivery retries. Stale revisions stop, temporary failures retry, and cancellation propagates.
- Review tasks use deterministic source reconciliation instead of model completion claims. Recovery handles blocked requests individually, and resolved sources repair interrupted card projections.
- Calendar reminders verify the current authorized occurrence and deliver a stable owner-facing reminder. Canceled/moved events and foreign recipients produce no message; event text cannot authorize work.
- Stale leadership assessments are distinct from invalid coverage evidence. Deploy the companion broker correction so preemption does not become a spurious evidence block.

- Saves authenticated owner focus and coverage decisions in platform-owned, revisioned records. Duplicate callbacks, missed events and restarts recover the same decision without inferring consent from prose.
- Adds evidence-based leadership assessment for General and Game Studio, canonical assessment cards, one unresolved proactive recommendation, native owner questions, fulfillment reassessment and continuation. External engagements remain unverified until supported evidence exists.
- Preserves active hiring across focus changes and fences predecessor installations after Chief replacement. Unknown evidence becomes visible blocked work with bounded model correction and recovery on relevant changes or review dates.
- Recommendation delivery retains intent and acknowledgment and retries stable message/action identities after a crash.
- Uses SDK 5.4.0. Sibling SDK and memory references now require explicit opt-in; shipping uses pinned packages. Existing onboarding and hiring-policy keys are retained.

- Ordinary discussion of Product Managers no longer creates staffing recommendations. Questions about hiring policy no longer reopen setup, and authenticated hiring answers are processed before preference-change requests.
- The one-question-or-staffing-change rule now also covers deterministic candidate selection after model tools finish. Required PM context delivery runs before the final reply.
- Product Manager updates use meaningful business, finance, workforce, objective and workstream content. Unchanged context is skipped; interrupted deliveries retain their original request and retry identity. The context-update workflow owns PM review; the unused extra plan call is removed.
- Approved role removal is restricted to its team and source-request lineage. Stale approval events cannot revive superseded requests. Product consultations require a stable message or turn identity, malformed conversation work is ignored, and host cancellation propagates.
- Removes three subscriptions that previously acknowledged notifications without handling them: communication mentions, management status reports and resource-need reports. Direct conversations, resource-change requests/decisions, incident handling and platform-scheduled calendar work continue through their supported paths. Durable manager-report intake remains future work.

Deploy SDK 5.4.0 and the companion C-Sweet additive migrations, five hidden leadership capabilities and hiring-card fixes before updating through Installed Agents. Review the five new grants in GRANTS.md. The platform cancels withdrawn hiring cards, completes fulfilled cards, activates eligible successors and repairs proven source-linked orphans. Historical recommendations and installation-scoped hiring policy are preserved; leadership grants do not expand hiring authority.

Proactive assessment defaults off (`CSweet:ChiefLeadership:Enabled=false`). Enable only after the installed published candidate and target-model/UI journeys meet G3. Pause through the rollout control or the explicit owner instruction before rollback, and retain the additive schema. Other profiles retain focus/onboarding but require their own proactive acceptance fixtures. Broad Phase 4/5 workflow changes remain behind G3. Local package verification does not constitute publication or deployment.
